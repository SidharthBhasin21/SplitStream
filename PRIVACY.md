# Privacy Policy for Lumenz

_Last updated: August 20, 2026_

Lumenz ("the app") is a collaborative expense-splitting app for small groups. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and how you can remove it.

## Information we collect

- **Account info**: name and email address, either entered directly at sign-up or provided by Google when you use "Sign in with Google."
- **Group and expense data**: group names, group membership, expenses, settlements, and loans you or other group members record — this is the core content of the app and is shared with the other members of your groups.
- **Push notification token**: if you allow notifications, a device push token is stored so we can notify you about group activity (new expenses, settlements). No notification content is stored beyond what's needed to deliver it.
- **Camera**: the camera is used only to scan a group's QR invite code. No photo or video is captured, stored, or uploaded — the camera feed is processed on-device to read the code and is immediately discarded.

We do not collect location data, browsing history, or advertising identifiers, and we do not run analytics or ad-tracking SDKs.

## How we use your information

Your data is used solely to operate the app's core features: authenticating you, syncing group and expense data in real time between members, calculating balances, and delivering activity notifications. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

## Third-party services

Lumenz is built on:

- **Supabase** — hosts our database, authentication, and real-time sync. Your account and group data is stored on Supabase's infrastructure.
- **Google Sign-In** — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google provides your name and email to us per their own privacy policy.
- **Expo Push Service / Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)** — used only to deliver push notifications to your device.

## Data retention and account deletion

You can permanently delete your account from **Profile → Delete account** in the app. This immediately and permanently removes your sign-in credentials (email/password or Google link) and your display name.

Expenses and settlements you recorded are **not** deleted — they remain in your groups' records (shown as "Deleted user") so that other members' balances stay accurate. This is the only data that is retained after deletion, and it can no longer be linked to your email, login, or name.

## Children's privacy

Lumenz is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
